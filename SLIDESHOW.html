What is a Ghost Cart?

Services don't need carts. They need Direct Action.

  • Eliminates traditional checkout steps.
  • Accepts payment without navigating to new pages.
  • Turns qualification directly into a transaction.

The Service Advantage

Why this is a game-changer for service businesses:

Ghost Cart
