The Ghost Cart Experience

What is it?

Services don't need carts. They need Direct Action.

  • Eliminates traditional checkout steps.
  • Payment without navigation.
  • Qualification → Transaction.

The Lead Filter

Ghost Cart Protects your time by driving away noise.

  • Early disqualification.
  • Hides checkout from non-serviceable leads.
  • Focus on ready-to-pay customers.
